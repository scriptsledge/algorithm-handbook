## 💼 LinkedIn Post (Professional & Clean)

How do you find the middle of a linked list in a single pass?

It is a classic interview question that tests your understanding of pointer manipulation and algorithm optimization.

That is the core challenge of LeetCode 0876 (Middle of the Linked List).

🔹 The Goal: Return the middle node of a singly linked list, or the second middle node if there are two.
🔹 The Solution: We use a "Two Pointers" approach to solve this in O(N).

• Step 1: Initialize slow and fast pointers at the head.
• Step 2: Move slow by one step and fast by two steps until fast reaches the end.

This optimization avoids the extra pass required to count the total number of nodes, making the solution more efficient.
