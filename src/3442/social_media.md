🔹 The Goal
Maximize freq(a1) - freq(a2) where a1 is odd and a2 is even.

🔹 The Solution
We use a "Frequency Counting" approach to solve this in O(N) time.

• Step 1: Count character frequencies using a fixed-size array.
• Step 2: Track the maximum odd and minimum even frequencies.

The beauty of this problem is its simplicity—by focusing on the extremes (max odd and min even), we arrive at the optimal solution instantly.
